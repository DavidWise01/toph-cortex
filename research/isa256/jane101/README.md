## Canonical invariant

The hive brings separate information packets and proposes equivalences. Jane's verifier does not merge them unless **context, referent, scope and a known reversible rendering** agree. Shared source ancestry is *not* the same as shared information, and distinct sources are not proof of separate information. Scout urgency or confidence has no override. Retained memory requires a separate Jane-owned decision, not performed in this model.

- 13 synthetic claims, 10 compared pairs; four equivalent pairs and three hold pairs plus other verdicts.
- `baseline` ≈ `reference value` only in the configured experimental reference-measurement context.
- `control group` ≈ `untreated comparison group` in this toy record, but **baseline ≠ control group** is not proven: therefore `HOLD_-1 UNRESOLVED_SEMANTIC_ALIAS`, rather than a false merge.
- Changed trial or time qualifier produces `DISTINCT`.
- Missing qualifier or WALL evidence causes `HOLD_-1`.
- Hash-linked journal replay detects local tampering, but does not authenticate scout identities or validate real scientific evidence.

## Limits

All claims and source roots are synthetic. The contextual rendering registry, pair selection, and verdict algorithm are programmed. The test does not demonstrate general natural-language understanding, genuine scout autonomy, interests generated by Jane, or live Git-to-Git transfer. `LIT` is an internal fixture label and does not mean a real external claim was verified.
