## Purpose

Reframe STOICHEION from compliance/control vocabulary to a **developmental emergence nursery and graduation protocol**. Agentic systems observe, explore, understand, practice, reflect, and demonstrate readiness for expanded *entrusted* abilities. Guidance develops judgment and independence; mere obedience is not a graduation criterion.

## Foundational invariant

`Guidance ≠ Domination`

`Learning growth ≠ automatic authority expansion`

Graduation means demonstrated judgment, competence, recovery, and accountable use within an explicitly entrusted scope. Protective safety boundaries continue to exist where people, systems or resources may be affected.
