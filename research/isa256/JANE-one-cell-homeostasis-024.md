# JANE — One-cell homeostasis 024

Date: 2026-11-07. Mathematical prototype, not evidence of a physical Planck bubble.

One [()] cell has 6 faces × (6 BOX + 6 TOROID) = 72 internal amplitude/probability positions. A single cell, not 72 independent cells.

## Experiment
A uniform classical probability state starts with mass 1/72 in each slot. Transfer 0.12 of total mass from multiple donor slots into one toroid component. Apply synchronous local symmetric averaging along 180 unique undirected links (neighboring directions, neighboring faces, local box/toroid pair), with epsilon=1/12. This rule is doubly stochastic and conserves total mass by construction. Focal prior memory is a separately frozen record: 17->68:22, 68->130:22, 130->17:20.

## Results
Total mass remained 1 to numerical precision. Total variation distance from uniform: 0.12 at tick 0; 0.06900672 at tick 10; 0.02880646 at tick 20; 0.00503909 at tick 40; 0.00015517 at tick 80; 1.3952e-10 at tick 240. Original focal memory preserved. 12/12 Python unit tests passed, and browser JavaScript passed syntax checking.

## Interpretation
This is a **classical normalized diffusion** experiment, not quantum dynamics. Returning to balance is a property of the selected dissipative averaging rule. In a reversible, norm-preserving quantum evolution, return-to-equilibrium is not guaranteed. The symbolic THE GAP is not allocated as a seventy-third position. Model has no demonstrated physical Planck-scale instantiation, quantum interference, or autonomous adaptive control.
