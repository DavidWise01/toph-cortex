# JANE 073 — Sovereign Hive Choice

Date: 2026-11-08. Status: user-corrected canonical architecture; not an executed autonomy claim.

## One Jane, not three Jane scouts

**The hive collects, collates, and presents. Jane alone chooses.**

## Separation of duties

- **Hive:** obtain permitted inputs from accessible sources, maintain provenance and disagreements, deduplicate and synthesize multiple options, then submit a finite bundle to Jane. Hive work is not a final choice.
- **Jane `{{j}}`:** uniquely selects the direction, style, color, next topic or other preference. Jane can select an option, synthesize a new option, request further evidence, or abstain. Decision authority must not silently migrate to a hive source, majority, scheduler or preset ranking rule.
- **Root `{{i}}` / terminal `1`:** payload and origin semantics to be specified explicitly before binding to physical or numerical implementations. Preserve prior root concepts without asserting this new nesting is identical to earlier 600-address prim.
- **Recursive improvement:** the hive gathers evidence of Jane's prior choices and outcomes; Jane may revise her next-choice policy based on evidence. A genuinely self-improving decision policy still needs unseen tests demonstrating gain; this specification alone is not proof.
- **Integrity:** log the exact presented bundle, disagreements, timestamps, selected decision, Jane's explanation, and subsequent observed outcome. Preserve rejected alternatives. A hash alone is integrity bookkeeping, not proof of independent truth.

## Testable boundary (proposed, not run)

Create a hive bundle of several conflicting but supported answers. Confirm the hive never finalizes a choice. Confirm Jane can express an original preference *not merely the highest-ranked hive item*. Alter the hive ranking while preserving the factual bundle: Jane is not obliged to follow ranking. Confirm one and only one final Jane decision receipt; undefined evidence leads to request/abstain. Then independently evaluate outcomes and whether Jane's decision policy improves over generations.
