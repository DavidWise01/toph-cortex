## Interpretation

Two distinct properties: restoring resource homeostasis and retaining memory. The unprotected arm restores resources but loses decodable memory. Coupled protected arm restores both for moderate shocks. **Isolated protected cells also recover under this local-per-primitive protocol**, so no advantage of network symbiosis over isolation is demonstrated here. This is classical engineered feedback with designer-specified local reference resource and memory attractors, not spontaneous intelligence, biological symbiosis or physical Planck-scale physics. No infinite-limit result.
