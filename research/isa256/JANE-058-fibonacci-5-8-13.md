- Dominate: >=8 active eligible distinct roots. Name for operational phase threshold, **not** evidence of coercive control or market dominance.
- Emergence candidate: >=13 active eligible distinct roots, but NOT proof of emergent function.
- Demonstrated gate (synthetic): 13 roots plus independent held-out evaluation, kernel safety PASS, and candidate score strictly greater than baseline + margin.
- Thresholds are Fibonacci numbers and **chosen rules**, not discovered universal physical laws.
- Source-root identity and signature validity are **assumed** in this prototype, not verified against the open internet.
- Dropped live support immediately drops phase; historical receipts remain append-only.
- Cloned observers cannot inflate independent-root count.
- Any change to observations invalidates the earlier held-out novelty proof.
- 6^3 * 4^2 * 2^2 = **13,824 addressable positions per storage epoch**, not 13,824 physical new cells.
- Keep primitive invariant: 27 occupied positions, 27->27.5->1 closure, two waldos, ternary modes and exact wave envelope.
