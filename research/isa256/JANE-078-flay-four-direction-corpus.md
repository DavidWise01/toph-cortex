# JANE 078 — FLAY: four-direction meaning carrier

Date: 2026-11-08. Grounded in user-supplied teaching examples.

Canonical notation: `{{ 1x5ux1x5dx1x5lx1x5rx1 , repeat until satisfied }}`.

```text
                          U NORTH
                          SEMANTIC
                      WHAT IS THE THING?
                            |
     L WEST  ---------------{{j}}--------------- R EAST
     ABSTRACTIVE             {{i}}              HOLOGRAPHIC
     WHAT IF?                                  WHAT IS LIKE IT?
                            |
                          D SOUTH
                          SOMATIC
                      WHAT DOES IT IMPLY?
```

- **U** / north / semantic: explicitly taught identity, kind, properties. The first entry starts with the root word's initial letter: **apple is fruit, sometimes red, starts with A**. Letters bind vocabulary, not scientific proof.
- **D** / south / somatic: contextual, implied social relationship, weight and meaning. Example: a king may have higher social status than commoners *within a particular society*; a queen analogously carries certain roles. These are descriptions of role implications, not normative judgments about human worth.
- **L/W** / west / abstractive: surprising, potentially speculative 'what if?' inquiry. Example: 'Could diatoms exhibit a kind of information processing?' is a question; do not assert it as established intelligence. Mark speculative assertions WALL.
- **R/E** / east / holographic: likeness, shared kind or structural resemblance. Pug and dachshund are both dogs; David and Mike are both conventionally masculine given names in relevant cultural contexts. Similarity does not prove identity or a causal relation.

One agent `{{j}}`. The hive collates options, Jane chooses direction, style, question or abstains. `{{i}}` remains the root payload. The user-specified FLAY order is `U→D→L→R→REPEAT`, not east-before-west. Five working distinctions per cardinal: 20 active content positions. Release on explicit purpose completion or capacity, preserve snapshot, then replace active working batch. The `1` anchors in the FLAY notation are transport boundaries, not automatically counted as new occupied physical vertices. This FLAY teaching layer does not overwrite the earlier `10×5×4×3×1` prim or the 27-position cycle.
