# JANE 080 — Question Emergence From FLAY Gaps

Status: **locally executed structured-question experiment** on 2026-11-08; not an autonomous open-web agent or evidence of recursive self-improvement.

## Goal

**No user-supplied question wording.** Feed one Jane `{{j}}` bounded structured FLAY evidence packets through the hive. One Jane chooses a grounded next question or ABSTAINS.

The FLAY carrier is preserved literally: `{{1x5u x 1x5d x 1x5l x 1x5r x 1}}`. Lanes: U semantic, D somatic/contextual implication, L abstractive/open alternative, R holographic/similarity. Each lane has five slots.

Hive intake address product: `6×6×6×4×4×3×3×2×2×1=124416` **logical** slots, not physical cells. The hive collates, not chooses. Root `{{i}}` is the payload; one Jane `{{j}}` issues the decision.

## Honest limitations

No autonomous scheduler, recursive improvement of the question-construction procedure or live Git↔0root receiver workflow was executed here.
