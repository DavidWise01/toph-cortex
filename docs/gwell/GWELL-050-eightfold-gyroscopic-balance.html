GWELL 050 — 4 Down + 4 Up

Eight 13-ring disks. Four downward-facing wells and four opposed upward-facing wells, around one cobalt FORMIC center. The pairing tests angular-momentum cancellation, not measured gravitational equilibrium. Parent: GWELL 049.

Opposing spin ratio 1.00 · Tilt mismatch 0° · 

Each disk uses 13 symbolic annular weights [1,1,1,1,1,1,1,1,2,3,5,8,13]. Opposed pairs have matching representative inertia I=39 and opposite spin signs. The four reference directions are radial in the projected x-y plane. The upper disk in each pair has a user-adjustable spin ratio and axis tilt mismatch. Total L is computed vectorially from I × omega × orientation and shown as a magnitude. Zero net L is necessary for the proposed cancellation, but not sufficient to prove dynamic or gravitational stability; external torque and actual material geometry are not modeled.
